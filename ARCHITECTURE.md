# Architecture

A map of the code for finding things fast. `GAME_DESIGN.md` says what to build; `CLAUDE.md` gives the rules. This file says where things live and what breaks easily. Written 2026-10-29 at `4bbd1c9` (after milestone 8); updated for milestones 9 to 16, the City's rework into a city on a hill (2026-11-09) and milestone 17 (Proving Ground, airbags, the chairlift riders take; 2026-11-15) milestone 18 (first person), milestone 21 (avalanches, the corner trail map) milestone 22 part 1 (the snowboard, 2026-11-16) and part 2 (the boards in the Locker, 2026-11-17), milestone 23 part 1 (the editors: side by side, custom boards, the painting lag; 2026-11-17) and part 2 (painted jackets and pants, the editors' fixed preview windows; 2026-11-17; then finer squares and the preview turned by hand, 2026-11-18), milestone 24 (the marketplace and the dev unlock, 2026-11-18). Line numbers rot, so prefer the function names.

## The big picture

```
maps/*.json ──registry (lazy import)──► parseMap ──► buildMap ──► BuiltMap { hf, course{rails,obstacles}, race, decor, starts, gaps }
                                                                   │                     │
                         sim (120 Hz, no Three.js scene) ◄─────────┘                     └──► WorldView (render)
                         SkierState ──(prev, cur, alpha)──► interpolateSkier ──► SkierView / ChaseCamera / SnowFx / audio
```

- **No event bus.** Scoring, snow effects and audio each diff `skierPrev` against `skier` themselves to find takeoffs, landings (`landTime` reset), crashes (`bailed` rising), pole plants or skating pushes (`poleTime` reset; snow effects watch it cross `polePlantTime`) and rail entry (`rail` going ≥ 0). Score events sit in a 16-slot ring that readers follow with their own cursor.
- **`TUNING`** (`sim/tuning.ts`) is what the sim reads: CONFIG × the ski's multipliers (`setSkiScale`), or on a snowboard the board type's (`setRide`, `CONFIG.boards[RIDE.type]`) × cheats, rebuilt only on change. It covers the `sim`, `skier`, `air`, `landing`, `rails`, `bail`, `slalom` and `giantSlalom` blocks. `score` and `checkpoints` read CONFIG directly, so cheats and skis don't touch them.
- **Invariant used at every milestone:** with every cheat off and the park ski, every `tools/sim` output stays byte-identical unless a change means to alter physics. A/B with `git stash push -- src tools`.
- **What the rider stands on (M22):** `RIDE` in `sim/tuning.ts` (`board`, `goofy`, and since part 2 `type`, the board type; `setRide(board, goofy, type?)`) is module state read by the sim (the board's edges, rail presses), scoring (names), `ui/keyNames.ts actionName`, `snowFx` (one track) and `audio/riding.ts`; `SkierView.setRide` swaps the rig. Main sets it from the outfit (`ride`, `stance`, `board`): at startup (after the `?board=regular|goofy|skis` override, which changes the outfit in memory only) and in `setOutfit`, which on a change of ride, stance or board type ends a combo under way, then calls `setRide`, `SkierView.setRide` and `LockerStage.setRide`. The dev hook `parkLaps.setRide(board, goofy, type?)` goes through `setOutfit(…, false)` (not saved).

### One frame (`main.ts`, `startLoop` callbacks)
- **Loop** (`core/loop.ts`): rAF re-arms first. Frame time is clamped to `sim.maxFrameTime` (0.25 s, so up to 30 catch-up steps), times `TUNING.sim.timeScale` (Slow motion). Nothing accumulates while `gameWaits()` (no level, a menu or the full trail map open, or graphics lost; the corner map never waits). Input edges latch until `sample`.
- **Step:** `keyboard.sample` → trail-map key (shows or hides the corner map, M21; no return) → R (tap respawns; holding freezes the sim, then `backToTop` after `checkpoints.holdToTop`) → confirm at finish → checkpoint key → `copySkierState(prev, skier)` → `stepSkier` → `stepAvalanches` (M21, a map with zones only; toasts on a release or a catch) → `sound.step` → `updateRun` → `stepScore` (if scoring) → `stepRace` (it can knock the skier's velocity *after* score and sound saw the step) → toasts → crash fade (unless `crashWait`).
- **Render:** `interpolateSkier` → `skierView.update` → orbit drag → `chase.update` → `world.view` (terrain streaming, culling) → `world.update` → `snow.update` (gets `skierView.skateSide`) → `lights.update` → `sound.frame` → `renderFrame` (backdrop pass, clear depth, world pass), or `drawLocker` while the Locker is open → HUD (and `cornerMap.update`, M21) → `auto.frame` → `tuningPanel.update`.

## Sim (`src/sim/`)
| File | Owns |
|---|---|
| `skier.ts` | `SkierState` and `stepSkier`. Phases are flags, not an enum: `bailed`, then `rail >= 0` (where `grounded` is also true), then `grounded` (snow), else air. Calls `stepBail`, `slideRail`, `rideSnow` / `fly` (forces), then `moveOnSnow` / `moveInAir` (move, collide, transition), `takeOff`, `land` (landing and bail check), `crash`, `catchRail` (from the air) / `mountRail` (from the snow), plus pops, butters and grabs |
| `terrain.ts` | `sampleSurface` / `groundAt`: bicubic Catmull-Rom on the grid **plus** `topShape` (exact analytic ramps, pipes and decks from `maps/shapes.ts`) |
| `rails.ts` | `buildRailShapes` (polylines into typed arrays), `railUnder` (snap), `railFrame`, `collideRails`, and for 3D rails `railUp` (the up, a smoothly blended direction and the bend at a distance along) |
| `obstacles.ts` | Capsules (trees, rocks, fences, walls) in an 8 m grid; `collideObstacles` and `insideObstacle`. Fences stand only as high as they look (`follow`, `rise`) |
| `bounds.ts` | `keepInside`: map rectangle plus the optional polygon |
| `powder.ts` | `inPowder`: off a map's runs and groomed patches (Freeride). A run marked `powder` (M15: the Steeps' and the Bowl's lines over the big cliffs) leaves its corridor powder. `Course.powder` carries it; `land` uses it for `landing.powderImpact`, and `snowFx` for spray and the carve sound |
| `checkpoints.ts` | `RunState`: flags, personal checkpoint, `respawnSkier`, finish zones |
| `race.ts` | Slalom courses: wand start, gate pass or miss, the finish, and spring-damper bending gates that `knock` the skier |
| `score.ts` | A pure observer: rotation from the `trick` quaternion delta in body axes, kept per axis as `Runs` each way (a reversal past `reverseMin` closes a run; each run counts its own whole steps, grace only on the last), combos and multipliers. `endGaps` scores gaps after `endAir`. `sketchyLanding` is shared with audio Since M16 it takes the map's `hf`: riding a wallride's wooden face (`shapeSurfaceAt` is `SURFACE.wood`, steeper than `score.wallrideAngle`) scores "Wallride" per meter when the skis leave it (`WallTrack`) |
| `lift.ts` (M17) | The chairlift riders take: `buildLifts` (from a `chairlift` line with `ride: true`) samples the cable's loop every meter (up the right-hand side, round each bullwheel), gives it a speed at every point (slow in the stations, fast on the line) and so a time table; chairs are evenly spaced in time, and `chairAt(path, clock, chair)` places any of them. `Course.lifts` carries the paths and the one clock (`stepLifts`, called at the top of `stepSkier`), which runs faster while the skier is out on the line (`lift.rideBoost`) |
| `avalanche.ts` (M21) | Avalanche zones from a map's `avalanches`: `buildAvalanches` traces each zone's lanes down the fall line on the built snow (from its `top` line, `LANE_ACROSS` 8 m apart, a point every `LANE_STEP` 2 m, smoothed by `FALL_KEEP`) and paints them into a 4 m lookup grid (lane and point per cell), so `zoneCell` is one lookup. `stepAvalanches` (after `stepSkier`, in main and in a harness `Rider` with `avalanches` on) runs each zone's phase (`LOADED` → `CRACKED` for `warning` s → `RUNNING`, the front building toward the zone's speed and slowing to a stop over its run-out → `SETTLED` → `LOADED` after `reload` s), rolls `chance` when the rider rides (grounded) onto a loaded zone, and catches a rider within the `body` m behind the front, under `depth` + `clearance` (No crashes: never), once per slide; then carries the crashed rider along (`carry`). `releaseZone`, `placeInAvalanches` (a respawn or placement isn't riding in), `zoneOutline`, `lanePoint` |
| `gaps.ts` | `buildGaps` (per map, in `BuiltMap.gaps`): each open gap jump's takeoff and landing areas ("Gap"), the map's named `gaps`, and fence segments from `fence` decor. Scoring reads it only at takeoff and landing (`clearsZone`, `crossesFence`) |

**Config:** `boards` (M22: each board type's multipliers, like `skis`; part 2: `park` is part 1's board, `freeride` and `carving` repeat its `skier` keys and add the big-mountain and carving skis' multipliers, so a change to the park board's edge numbers must be copied to the other two; `skier.toeEdge`/`heelEdge` are 1 and only a board reads them), `airbag` and `lift` (M17) and `avalanche` (M21; read straight from CONFIG, not TUNING), (`score.wallrideAngle`, `wallrideMin`, `wallrideMax`, `wallridePoints` since M16) `skier`, `air`, `landing` (`caseTrickMax`, `casePitchMax`, `powderImpact` since M9), `rails` (`uprightCos` since M14), `bail`, `slalom`, `giantSlalom`, `score` (`loopPoints`, `corkscrewPoints`, `loopTurn` since M14), `checkpoints`, `sim`, `cheats`, `skis`.

**Fragile**
- **Airbags (M17):** an `airbag` feature is a ramp whose every stretch is `SURFACE.airbag`; `setShapes` sets `hf.airbags`, and only then does the sim look (`onAirbag`, a `shapeSurfaceAt` call) at landings, head-first hits, hard bumps and, for `airbag.grip` s after a landing there, each step on the snow (the pad's drag). On a map with none, nothing new runs: every tool stayed byte-identical. `land` on a pad never crashes (the No crashes path: upright along the travel), keeps `airbag.keep` of the speed and sets `landSoft`, which `sketchyLanding` returns true for (half points, no multiplier, no cheer) and scoring tags `'airbag'`. `landSoft` is cleared wherever `landPowder` is.
- **The chairlift (M17):** `SkierState.lift` (the lift, or -1), `liftChair` (the chair; -1 waiting at the loading point; -2 sliding off at the top) and `liftT`. `rideLift` runs instead of everything else in `stepSkier`: `grounded` stays true, the position is the chair's (from the same clock the renderer reads, so nothing drifts), and `velocity` is just the change of position. Anything that takes `grounded` as "on the snow" must also check the lift: `updateRun` resets the run instead (no flags, no finish), `audio/riding.ts` and `snowFx` skip the snow sounds, spray and tracks, `SkierView` skips `sitOnDrawnSnow`. `enterGate` takes any skier within `lift.gateRadius` of the loading point going slower than `lift.gateSpeed`. The clock lives on the course, not the skier, so a respawn doesn't move the chairs.
- **Avalanches (M21):** the slides' state lives on the course (`Course.avalanches`), like the lift's clock: a respawn doesn't reset a slide, a map load starts every zone loaded. Only `stepAvalanches` moves them, and the headless harness calls it only when a tool sets `Rider.avalanches` (as `popWithUp` keeps ↑ popping), so every older tool rides Freeride's steeps exactly as before (every output stayed byte-identical). Rolls come from a seeded random on the course (`avalanche.seed`), so tools are repeatable. A zone only sets off on the snow (`grounded`): flying over its top and landing inside counts as riding in, appearing there (respawn) doesn't (`placeInAvalanches`, called by main's `respawn`). A crash by a slide is `bailReason` `'avalanche'` (`buryInSlide` in `skier.ts`, which uses `crash()` and its `contactNormal` scratch), and main waits `avalanche.respawnDelay` (3 s) before fading instead of `bail.respawnDelay`. Zones are traced on the finished heightfield at build, so any terrain change moves them (rerun `freeride.ts` and `tools/sim/avalanche.ts`). Flags 4, 5, 7, 23, 24 and 25 sit on zone paths: respawning there never sets one off, but a slide already running can still reach it.
- **Rails are solid only 2.5 m under their tops (M17,** `PARAPET_DEPTH` in `sim/rails.ts`, once the viaduct parapet's own rule): a rail can span a gap high over riders (Proving Ground's Slot). Every tool stayed byte-identical.
- **Snowboard (M22):** one sim; every board difference is behind `RIDE.board`, so skis run exactly the old code (all 88 baseline outputs byte-identical). The sim's `yaw` and `orient` are the board's (-Z its nose); nothing in the sim knows the rider faces across it except `rideSnow`'s two edges: the heel edge is the board's left riding regular (lean > 0), its right goofy; the carve's grip eases from one edge's multiplier to the other's over `BOARD_EDGE_LEAN` (0.15 rad, so nothing jumps at flat), and edge hold uses the uphill edge (by `sidePull`'s sign). On a rail a board runs `updateButter` (a nose or tail press: a pose and a name only; the pop out of it gets `butterPop`, a nollie off the rail); skis still `endButter`. Landings are unchanged: a board across its travel fails `sideMax` like skis, fakie counts as lined up. Lincoln loops roll about the board's length (wildcat/tamedog) and flips (↑/↓) turn end over end (back and front flips), so the physics needed no new axis.
- **Board trick names (`sim/score.ts`, M22):** `side(dir, fakie)` names a spin's first counted run (`firstDir`, set by `spinRuns` beside `counted`) frontside when dir × stance × (fakie ? -1 : 1) > 0 (a left turn is frontside riding regular forwards); `loopName` gives wildcat/tamedog. Rails: `beginRail` (now given `prev` and the rails) works out `RailTrack.cameFrom` from the air or snow velocity across the rail at the catch (over `CAME_ACROSS` 0.6 m/s), else the side of its line (`CAME_OFF` 0.06 m), else 0 (straight on: a boardslide); `trackRail` sorts sideways meters into `slides` by which end crossed (the nose pointing away from where the rider came from is a boardslide) and which way the chest faces; `boardSlideName` picks the most meters. A slide name the HUD shows every frame (`liveSlide`) allocates strings, as the ski's always did.
- Adding a `SkierState` field means editing `createSkierState`, `copySkierState` and `interpolateSkier` (`render/skierView.ts`) together. Nothing catches a miss. (`interpolateSkier` copies only what rendering reads; `landPowder` and `trickTurn` are sim-only.)
- **Pop vs ↑ (M9):** the sim crouches and pops on `input.pop` and front-flips on `input.up`, except while a pop is still possible with `pop` held. "Pop with: ↑" is `mirrorPop` (`core/input.ts`) copying `up` into `pop` after each sample, in `Keyboard.sample` and in the harness (`Rider.popWithUp`, default true), which is what keeps every tool byte-identical. Anything new that reads ↑ on the snow must read `pop` instead.
- **Case landings (M9):** `land` allows `casePitchMax` while `trickTurn` (rotation summed over the air, reset in `takeOff`) is under `caseTrickMax`. Not the `trick` quaternion's angle: a full flip returns it to identity. `sketchyLanding` uses the powder impact limit when `landPowder`.
- `yaw` is frozen at its takeoff value in the air; `land` re-derives it from the body. `interpolateSkier` special-cases this (the switch-landing snap was fixed there), and `race.ts placeBody`, the headlamp and the camera all see the stale yaw mid-air.
- Module-level scratch vectors are shared between functions: `land` / `crash` read the `contactNormal` that `moveInAir` just set, and `railSpot` writes `head`, `railDir` (and `railAlong` on a 3D rail). Reordering calls breaks things silently.
- **3D rails (M14):** a rail whose points carry `up` (styles `loop` and `corkscrew`) has `RailShape.ups` and `bend` (curvature along the up, from the polyline at build time). `railUnder` and `collideRails` skip its segments that aren't upright (`rails.uprightCos`): seen from above it folds back over itself, so it can only be caught, ridden onto or bumped on its upright stretches. `slideRail` takes the normal from `railUp` and the heading in the rail's own frame (`headingOn3d`: `yaw` is then the heading after tilting to the normal, like on the snow, so only `orient` says where the body points), and past upright lets go (`takeOff`) when `railSpeed² × bend + g × up.y < 0`; on upright stretches it never lets go, so the ride-on kinks hold like any rail. The physics moves along the straight segments (`railDir`), but the up and the body follow `railUp`'s smoothly blended direction (`railAlong`): squaring the up to each segment rocked it at every point of a corkscrew. A pop off one goes along its up. `SkierState.rail3d` marks it; `SkierView` then draws `orient` (not tilt × yaw), `ChaseCamera` rolls, and scoring sums the normal's turn (`RailTrack.turn`) for "Loop" / "Corkscrew" (`score.loopTurn`). Every other rail runs the old code untouched (the tools stay byte-identical).
- `air.wallAngle` (44°) decides what counts as a wall. That covers pops, air leveling and how every pipe, gully and lip is authored. Takeoff faces are exempt, and a pop off a lip steeper than 44° goes straight up.
- Landing limits (`landing.*`, scaled per ski) also set "sketchy" in scoring and whether the crowd cheers. Changing a limit changes scores and audio too.
- **Scoring reads rotation in runs (M10).** `spinRuns` and the flip loop in `endAir` iterate closed runs then the one going, and leave the degrees counted in the module-level `counted` (read right after the call; `butterName` relies on it too). A closed run gets no `spinShort`/`flipShort` grace, so a reversal only counts when the first run turned a full step. Which axis names a trick is decided by `runPath` (every run's size summed), not the net angle.
- **Gaps (M10)** are built from map data in `sim/gaps.ts`: open gaps from `JumpFeature` geometry duplicated from `maps/shapes.ts` (`lip`, `knuckle`, the landing's end, +60 m `LANDING_PAST`), fences only from `fence` line decor (not netting or barriers), and the takeoff point is `prev.position` at `beginAir`. Jam Park's Cat gap polygons come from `tools/maps/jampark.ts OVER`, so moving that jump means rerunning the generator. Numbers outside config: `TAKEOFF_BEFORE`, `TAKEOFF_PAST`, `LANDING_PAST`, `MAX_RUNS`.
- `race.ts` reads `CONFIG.render`'s lean numbers and hard-codes body heights to place the gate-hit body. Render tuning changes gameplay here, and it can drift from what `skierView` draws.
- `slideRail` skips `keepInside`, `bump` and obstacles. `CHEATS.noCrashes` is checked in four places in `skier.ts`. The obstacle grid bakes `rails.bodyRadius` at build time, so a tuning-panel change to it doesn't apply.
- Linear scans every step: all rails (with a bounding-box cull), all gates in `bendSides`, and `pipeAt` / `quarterInward` over all shapes (long on Freeride's town).
- **Rail grid (M16):** `railUnder` and `collideRails` visit only the rails listed in a 16 m grid cell (`railGrid`, built once per rails array, kept in a `WeakMap`), each rail listed in every cell its bounding box comes within `RAIL_GRID_REACH` (4 m) of, in index order, so results match the full scan exactly (every tool stayed byte-identical). A reach over `RAIL_GRID_SAFE` (2 m, e.g. from the tuning panel) scans them all. A step on the City went from 8 to 9 µs to about 3.
- Numbers outside config: `CREASE_COS`/`CREASE_LEAVE`, the bail stick of 0.25, `LOG_RADIUS`/`TUBE_RADIUS`, the obstacle `CELL`/`SINK`, bounds margins, and score `SIDEWAYS`.
- The sim imports Three.js `Vector3` and `Quaternion` (math only, which is allowed).

## Maps (`src/maps/`, generators in `tools/maps/`)
- **Pipeline:** `registry.ts` (`MapId` union + `MAPS`, one lazy chunk per JSON) → `loader.ts parseMap` (hand-written validation and defaults; **unknown keys are dropped silently**, and nothing is fetched here) → `build.ts buildMap`: `generateTerrain` → `startFrames` → `setShapes` (ramps, pipes, starts, town) → `buildRailShapes` (after the shapes, so rails sit on them) → `layoutDecor` → `buildObstacles` → `buildRace`.
- **Terrain is a recipe, never stored heights.** `generated` (`terrain.ts`: a pitch profile, lanes solved to end level, berms, hills) or `peak` (`peak.ts`: spokes from a summit, Hermite-blended, then ordered `elements` such as ridge, gully, cliffs, lip, bowl, pillows, bumps, dip, flat and (M15) track, plus a rim at the bounds). A `heightfield` type is planned for M19 (Brundage, on hold).
- **Shapes** (`shapes.ts`) are exact Hermite profiles on top of the grid in 32 m cells. The highest one wins. Cliffs are terrain elements; booters are `kicker`/`jump` with `natural: true`.
- `types.ts` holds the whole format (`MapDef`, `Feature` union, `TerrainDef`, `GapDef` for named scoring gaps). `decor.ts` does scatter and thinning (`thinDecor` per preset). Decor sets are terrain sets (`spacing`), area sets (`area`), and since M15 spots sets (`spots`: one item at each given spot, Freeride's forest edges along its runs). `layoutDecor` also collects `cornices` (peak `lip`s with `cornice`) for the renderer. Keep-out zones (`keepOutZones`) clear decor off flags, finishes, starts, features' run-ins and landings, runs, signs, lifts, props, and since M15 every named gap's way in, gap and landing (`GAP_RUN_IN`, `GAP_CLEAR`) and cliff bands with a `clear` (from `CLIFF_RUN_IN` above the bench to `clear` past the foot). `town.ts` builds buildings and terraces; `startGates.ts` builds the deck, tower, ridge and race house.

**Avalanche zones (M21):** `MapDef.avalanches`, a list of `AvalancheDef` (`name`, `top`: a line across the slope's top, left to right facing down; `length` m down its fall lines; `depth`, `speed`, `runout`; the loader defaults 1, 24 and 150). A zone is the ground swept by the fall lines from `top`, not a hand-drawn polygon: the sim traces them on the built snow (`sim/avalanche.ts`). Freeride's are written by `freeride.ts` (`avalanche()`; the chutes' inside the East block, the rest after it), last in `mapJson` so rerunning changed the JSON only by the new key; its report prints each zone's lanes and steepness and the flags on its path. Check with `tools/sim/avalanche.ts`.

**Feature kinds:** adding one (as M17's `airbag`) touches `types.ts` (the interface, `Feature`), `loader.ts parseFeature`, `shapes.ts buildShapes` (or `sim/rails.ts`), `decor.ts keepOutZones`, `render/features.ts`, and whatever the sim does on it.

**Adding a map** means touching `registry.ts` (`MapId` and `MAPS`), `ui/mapArt.ts` (`ART`), and a generator. With a leaderboard, also `online/limits.ts` (`COMBO_BOARDS`) and `online/boards.ts` (`BOARDS`), then redeploying the Worker by hand. Add its named gaps to `tools/sim/score.ts`'s last section (after the old summary, so older lines stay identical).

**Proving Ground (M17, `tools/maps/proving.ts`):** a resort with a contest venue at its foot, on a `generated` slope. Its big shapes are terrain lanes, not features: a lane that climbs, runs with the slope and drops is a mound whose banks (the lane's `blend`) are landings. The Slot is two such mounds 12 m apart (`SLOT`); the Hip is one narrow spine with 26 m banks (`HIPS`), kickers angled off its deck, a quarterpipe facing each bank; the Big One is Jam Park's pro-jump lane. Tree islands (`island()`) are laid in 16 m pieces whose edges are pulled in by up to `wander` m (never out, so trails keep their room), each piece its own seed, and `rib` raises a lane of ground under one. Trees in a tree gap are `spots` with `anywhere: true` (they'd otherwise be cleared off the jump). The School Yard's four lanes are a kicker and an `airbag` each, the pad's wall starting `BAG_BACK` before the lip so its top begins at the lip. The profile carries one more section past the map's end, falling away, or the land beyond would climb for good and stand as a wall across the valley. Named gaps' areas are laid so their middles line up with the takeoff's heading (the Slot's and the Hip's angled kickers): `tools/sim/score.ts` rides from one middle toward the other.

**The City (M16, a city on a hill since 2026-11-09; `tools/maps/city.ts`, kit in `tools/maps/cityKit.ts`):** a `peak` hill: the summit plaza (`flat`, r 50) walled by `rampart` stretches with a `turret` either side of each of eight gates, ring roads as `track` elements (they wind 3 times round, `RINGS`), four finish squares (`flat`) round the foot, and (before the terrain is built, since it's an element) the old town's sunken street (a `gully` down the North street under the viaduct). Radial streets (`streets`: 8 from the gates, 8 more from the first ring, 16 more from the third) wind by `amp`/`wave`; the blocks between two streets and two rings (`blocks`) each get a **polar frame**: x across from the block's middle in meters, d down from its top, mapped onto the hill (`K.Frame`). Everything in `cityKit.ts` is written in that frame and `put` maps it to the world (points one by one, single spots turned by the frame's heading there), so templates written for a straight slope work in a block that curves round the hill. A block's edge (`edgeX`) follows its street, so columns of buildings curve with it; each building is straight between its ends (`yawBetween`). Templates (`oldTown`, `stairStreet`, `rooftops` (`roofRow`), `alley`, `courtyard`, `houses`, `warehouses`, `towerBlock`) are picked by face and band (`template`); landmark blocks (`special`, `landmarkBlock`: the cathedral, the parish church, the civic hall, the station, the garage with the Garage gap, the building site, the park with the fountain) come first, then `infill` packs every block's leftovers (5 m alleys, shop, house or mid-rise by face). After the blocks: the plaza, finishes, flags and runs, then street life (trams on tracks round the second ring and buses at shelters on the first and third, each with a plow pile up to its roof (`vehicle`), plow trucks, furniture and people on the sidewalks, traffic lights and signs at crossings, the elevated line, the funicular, a skybridge between the two nearest tall towers), then lamps, then (with their own random, after the lamps so they keep clear of them) parked cars in rows of two to four along the ring roads' curbs, off streets, runs and the squares (`carAt`, laid the way the road falls), and the finish squares: a car park either side of the arch's way past the boulevard, a crowd at the arch, the square's name on a sign, two big spruces past the finish zone's clear snow, and a row of mid-rises along the foot of the bank round the square, outside the play area (`inBounds`).
- **Clear zones (`cityKit.ts` `keeps`, `footprints`):** `put` records every building's outline (with its drift and landing drift) and the clear snow every feature needs (`keepClear`: a terrace's run-in and 34 m past its front, a bank's or kicker's landing, a rail's or box's line and 14 m past it, a wallride's open side 20 m out and 36 m past its end, a drift's 12 m run-in, a landing drift's 40 m run-out). Columns (`clearFor`), rideable shops (`shop` returns null), strips (`blockedAt`, buildings and wallride zones), infill, trees, lamps, vehicles, furniture and people (`free`) all keep off them. The end of the script reports features inside buildings, buildings on ring roads and clear zones with a building in them.
- **Look (render):** landmark styles are drawn in `render/cityStructures.ts` (`drawLandmark`: `church`, `clock`, `civic`, `deco`, `twist`, `depot`, `rampart`, `turret` stand level like mid-rises; `garage` and `site` are ridden, their floors following the roof), line structures (`drawCityLine`: `viaduct`, `elevated`, `skybridge`, `funicular`, `tramTracks`, `scaffold`, with a `height` on `LineDecor`) and the props `crane` and `fountain`. Mid-rises (`block`) got a cornice, a string course, shop awnings and rooftop bulkheads, vents, water tanks or a rooftop garden (`render/town.ts`, `roofDetails`). Wallrides have the style `wall` and a `color`: drawn as the building's wall (stone courses, a pale coping, its plinth); the sim treats them as wood (`QuarterShape.wood`), so they score "Wallride".
- **Converted props (`render/propModels.ts`, `propModels.json`):** cars, the dumpster, hydrants, bins, traffic lights, signs and a water tower come from two CC0 packs (KayKit City Builder Bits, Quaternius LowPoly Modular Street) converted by `tools/models/packs.ts` (zips in the gitignored `reference/packs/`, read with its own zip, PNG, glTF and OBJ readers): flat triangles, 16-bit positions, a color per triangle sampled from the pack's palette texture or material, faces pointing up turned to snow, wheels left out (buried). The JSON is its own chunk (39 kB gzipped), fetched by `loadMap` only for a map that uses it (`needsPropModels`); without it the boxes fall back to the old procedural looks. Boxes stretch a model to their length, width and top (`addModelBox`); new box styles `hydrant`, `bin`, `newsBox`, `bollards`, `bikeRack`, `shelter`, `bus`, `tram`, `plow` (procedural) and `parapet` (drawn by the viaduct) are all slid and bonked like any box.

**Fragile (City)**
- `strip` keeps `CLEAR_BEFORE` (26 m) of open snow before anything to pop onto and `AFTER_LAUNCH` (24 m) between stairs or drops; bonks stand `OFF` (2.5 m) off the strip's middle; ledges `LEDGE.off` (4 m) out. Tighter, and `features.ts`'s riders run into their sides.
- **The checkers' start distances set the layout:** `features.ts` rides a building with no drift from 15 m behind its uphill wall (so a roof row's roofs are 16 m: the one before the last must hold the start), a terrace from 8 m behind its back to 33 m past its front (the terrace's clear zone, and 9 m between a landmark and its stair plaza), a wallride from 6 m out at 25° and 45°; `score.ts` rides a named gap from 12 m before its takeoff zone (the lip roof is 16 m, so the start is past the step down onto it). Kinds go to `features.ts` as separate arguments (`handrail jib`, not `handrail,jib`, which runs nothing).
- In a roof row each roof's uphill eave stands lower than the roof before it; a row too short for its alley gap is laid without the alley (`roofRow`'s `spots(false)`); the gap zones are taken from the roofs as laid (`roofRect`), since the street's edge curves.
- Wallrides go on a building's own wall with `wallrideOn` (world coordinates from the laid building), never on the street's edge line: the edge splays out down a block (up to 22° in the old town) and a wallride laid along it ran into its wall. Full length (18 m) only on buildings long enough (`WALLRIDE.before + length + past`).
- The viaduct's parapets are boxes of style `parapet`, high over the sunken street: `sim/rails.ts collideRails` makes that style solid only 2.5 m under its top (`PARAPET_DEPTH`), or they'd wall off the street under the bridge. No other map has one.
- Each part of the city draws from its own random (`K.useRandom`: blocks `1000 + i`, infill `5000 + i`, the plaza 7, street life 4242): a change in one block doesn't re-roll the rest. `GAP_NAMES` places the alley gaps by face and band; a landmark (`special`) takes its block first.
- Models stretched over a box (`railMesh.ts addModelBox`): the box frame's `right` is the model's -x (right × up = -along), so x is flipped going in; without it every car, dumpster, hydrant and bin was mirrored and drawn inside out.
- A level landmark's own stone stops just under its roof's snow (`top` - 0.15), or the stone's top face wins over the snow drawn at the same height (the civic hall's roof showed brown). The snow is the building's level roof over its whole footprint, so a landmark's walls must fill it: the church is a hall church (aisles as tall as the nave); the art-deco and twisted towers, which step in or turn as they rise, leave that roof undrawn (`maps/town.ts buildingShapes`) and draw their own snow on each setback.
- A finish zone keeps decor (trees, scatter) out for its whole length from its (x, z), 90 m out from the square's middle here: the squares' big spruces stand past it (`FOOT + 53`). Props and features aren't kept out.
- Block runs (`… : blocks …`) chain each block's open line across the ring roads; the headless rider drifts a few meters off them, so a few crash and restart (into stair plazas' side walls). The eight main-street runs are the clean check.

**Rail kit (M14):** (Freeride places its pieces down any fall line with `placeKit` in `freeride.ts`.) `tools/maps/railKit.ts` holds the rail shapes and set-ups the Park, Jam Park and Test slope scripts share: `SHAPES` (wave, A-frame, double kink, flat-down-flat-down, each ridden on from the snow), `poleJam`, `wallrideCloseOut` (a rail at 35° ending at a wooden wall's foot), `gapRail` (a rail stopping in the air short of a `landing` feature, plus its named "Rail gap"), `deckRail` / `knuckleRail` (to one side of a table: along its deck, or from the knuckle down the face, `follow: 1`), and the 3D `loop` and `corkscrew`.

**Fragile**
- Never hand-edit generated JSON: edit the script and rerun (`node tools/maps/x.ts`).
- **Feature order is identity for the tools:** `features.ts` reports rails by index and the sim's rails follow feature order. New features go after the older ones (the parks collect their table rails in `deckTables` / `knuckleTables` and lay them at the end), and new rails sit where riders coming off older ones don't run onto them (in the jib lines, 4 m either side of the middle), so older rails' outputs stay byte-identical. Generators bake in terrain-derived numbers (Freeride's booter yaws and log heights, finish widths), so a change to `peak.ts` or `terrain.ts` means rerunning them. Nothing checks for drift.
- Tree seeds count the decor before them (`seed: 100 + decor.length` in slalom and bigair): add new decor **after** the trees. Big air's shared `rand()` is consumed by spectators first. Changing a scatter's `count` or `area` re-places the whole set.
- `peak.ts` element order matters: a `flat` pad samples the elements before it. Bounds must be star-shaped from the summit.
- **Freeride's steeps (M15):** the North and East spokes' pitches were reshaped and the lower faces eased so each face still drops as far in all (the finishes and valley floors stay put); a change there moves everything below it. The East chutes are laid along the fall line of `groundSoFar()` (the ground near the summit from the elements laid before them), so an element added before them moves them. A cliff band's `clear` (on every band of 12 m or more, `STEEPS_CLEAR`) keeps decor off its bench, face and landing (`keepOutZones` in `maps/decor.ts`); without it, boulders on the benches and crags at the lips stop riders short. Today's bench shape (2.5 × the drop + 8 m) lands 25 m onto 38 to 45° powder at 18.6 m/s or less at any speed; the cap is about 30 m on 40° and 35 m on 43°. Groomed snow (a run without `powder`) still caps drops near 9 m. `tools/sim/cliffs.ts` is the check.
- **Cat tracks (M15):** a `track` element is a road whose height is the ground under its middle (from the elements before it, sampled every `TRACK_STEP`) averaged over `smooth` m, kept `crossfall` of the slope across it (Freeride 0.35; level made dropping onto it on a 30° face an impact crash), with straight banks back to the slope; it is always snow. It must come after anything that reshapes the ground under it: bumps added after a track corrugate the road. Freeride traces each road at `TRACK.grade` across the real slope of `groundSoFar()` built with the real bounds and rim, so every element before the tracks moves them; the named cat gaps (`gaps`, 400) sit where the face's main run comes nearest, with a 2 m kicker lip (`CAT_KICK`) on the cut bank's top edge. Side hits are `lip` elements turned 30° back toward their run, laid after the tracks.
- Authoring errors throw at build time (gap ≤ 1.5 m, pipe sections too long, a lane that can't get back level).
- Freeride's build is the heaviest (~38 MB of typed arrays, ~2M decor candidates). That matters on 4 GB machines. M15 took its scatter from 130k to 156k items and its obstacles from 89k to 109k (tree islands, krummholz, deadfall, boulder fields, outcrops, forest edges, a snag band).
- `runway`'s strips are lines of 4 m circles 4 m apart: a half-width that gives only two lines (over 4, up to 8) leaves uncovered spots down the middle, where decor still lands (a boulder on the Glades cat gap's way in did). Use a half-width of 10 or more, or 4 or less.
- A terrain set's clumps can't thin below about 15% cover (`clear` 1 sets the level at 0.35): to make a set sparse, narrow its height or slope band or widen its spacing.
- Numbers outside config: `HILLSIDE_RISE`, `RIM_HOLD`, `SHAPE_CELL`, `PIPE_WALL_START`, start-gate sizes, `CHUNK`.

- **Quota math (a class of 30, one lesson):** say each student opens 20 browse pages (kinds, orders, More), searches 5 times, uses 5 items, shares 2 and reports 1. Reads: 600 page views at up to 12 documents each would be 7,200 reads uncached; a class mostly opens the same first pages, and with the minute's cache about a quarter miss: about 1,800. Searches: 150, mostly different words, up to 12 each: about 1,800. Shares: 60 × 2 counter reads = 120. Reports: 30. About **3,750 reads** a lesson (9,150 at worst, with no cache hits). Writes: uses 150, shares 60 × 3 = 180, reports 30: about **360 writes**. On Spark (50,000 reads, 20,000 writes a day, shared with the leaderboards) that's about 13 lessons a day (5 with no cache hits at all); writes are never the limit. Workers Free (100,000 requests a day): about 40 requests a student a lesson (each POST's preflight is cached a day), 1,200 a lesson, about 80 lessons. Storage: items are 0.1 to 12 kB (typically 1 to 6), so 1 GiB holds over 100,000; the day counters are about 100 bytes each and nothing deletes them. When a quota runs out Firestore answers 429, the Worker a 502: the Market says offline and the game goes on.

## Audio (`src/audio/`)
- `engine.ts`: one AudioContext, made on the first key, click or touch (a capture listener registered before `Menus`). Buses: game and ui into sfx into master. The game bus fades while the game waits. `visibilitychange` suspends. One-shots are capped (`maxOneShots`); continuous `Voice`s update only past an epsilon.
- `synth.ts`: every sound is a recipe rendered once by `OfflineAudioContext` with a seeded RNG (each recipe its own seed, so adding one changes no other): 19 one-shots (`slabCrack`, M21: a crack splitting across the slope and the snowpack's deep settling thump; `footPush`, M22: a board's back boot stamping and scuffing) and 5 loops, done sequentially on the main thread at the first gesture. `skate` (M11) is the poles-off push. `bail` (M11) is a bone-crack click, a breathy "sh—" cut off at 0.2 s by a 1 kHz censor bleep to 0.6 s, and the slide fading; `BLEEP` holds its timing and levels against the crack, which `renderShot` normalizes, so the crack sets the loudness (shot gain 0.55: about -6 dBFS, like a soft landing) and the bleep's sine renders at -22 dBFS.
- `riding.ts`: diffs sim steps (pop, landing, rail, pole or skate (`poles`, set through `GameAudio.setPoles` from the outfit), or `footPush` on a board (`RIDE`), bail); on a board the carve and brake voices take `audio.board`'s gain and frequency (one long edge) and sets the carve, brake, slide, wind and rail voices each frame. `ambience.ts`: weather bed and howl (following `SnowFx.gust()`), crowd, town and lift places, cheers and jeers; since M21 avalanches (`updateSlides`): `slabCrack` when a zone's `releases` count changes (by its distance, out to `audio.avalanche.crackFar`), and a rumble (brown noise through a low-pass, a `Voice` like the weather bed) following the nearest running front, opening up close. `index.ts` (`GameAudio`) wires these and the menu click and tick. `wav.ts` is dev-only WAV export (`parkLaps.audio.renderWavs()`, written through `tools/vite/wavSink.js`).
- **Config:** `audio.*`. Many filter, Q and glide numbers and every recipe constant are hard-coded in the audio files.
- **Fragile:** the first gesture renders 23 recipes on the main thread (a likely hitch on a Celeron). Changing any part of the `bail` recipe louder than the crack changes what normalization scales to, and so the bleep's level: re-measure the WAV (the bleep's 1 kHz component, not the window's peak, since the slide sits under it). Audio reads render state (`snow.gust()`, `inPowder`). Pan uses the previous frame's camera. Sounds during steps before the context runs are dropped. Levels were set from WAV measurements, not by ear.

## App glue (`src/main.ts`, 889 lines)
- **Start gate** (`src/boot.ts`, the page's only entry): until the first click, tap or key press (not Tab, Esc or a lone modifier) nothing else is fetched or run: no Three.js, no WebGL, no loop. That key is swallowed (it doesn't press Play). Then it shows "Loading…" and `import('./main.ts')`; main removes `#boot` as before. A failed import says so and waits for another click. `?map=` skips the gate. Keep `boot.ts` tiny and import-free.
- The composition root (a lazy chunk loaded by `boot.ts`). Startup order: settings → outfit and ski scale → quality and weather (URL first) → `createView` (fatal message on failure) → context-loss handler → HUD, `GameAudio`, `ScoreHud`, `Keyboard` → `Menus` and every screen → dev hooks → `openTitle` → `?map=` → `startLoop`.
- `loadMap`: dispose the old world → `buildMap` → `WorldView` → run and race → `sound.setMap` → re-point `SkierView` and `ChaseCamera` (made once) at the new `hf` → weather → `warm` and shader compile behind "Loading" → close the menus.
- **Dev hooks** (`import.meta.env.DEV` only): `window.parkLaps` has `skier`, `teleport(x, z, heading, speed)`, `lookFrom`, `heightAt`, `loadMap`, `setQuality`, `setWeather`, `setCheat`, `snowFx`, `audio`, and more.
- **Fragile:** top-level `let`/`const` read by later closures (`level`, `tuningPanel`, `NO_RAILS`). Moving code upward causes temporal-dead-zone errors. `loadMap` mixes sim, render, audio, storage and UI.

## Tools (`tools/`; run from the repo root with `node tools/<dir>/<file>.ts`, needs Node ≥ 22.18 for type stripping)
- **Not type-checked:** `tsconfig` includes only `src` and `vite.config.ts`, so `npm run build` never checks `tools/`. Drift shows up only at runtime.
- `sim/harness.ts`: `loadMap(file)` runs the real `parseMap` + `buildMap`. `Rider` steps the real `stepSkier`, `updateRun` and `stepRace` with scripted keys. `--ski park|big|carving` works on every tool, and since M22 `--board [regular|goofy]` (regular when the word is left out) rides a snowboard: the board's multipliers, edges, rail presses and trick names; `--board-type park|freeride|carving` (part 2) picks the board (the park board without it, and the same header line). `Rider.avalanches` (M21, default false) runs the course's slides after each step (`cracked` says how many zones broke). `jumpLayout` duplicates `maps/shapes.ts` geometry by hand.
- Checkers (stdout only):
  - `flips.ts [map] [jump] [speed] [--tricks back,front,spin]`: the landing windows for letting go of a trick key (default the Park's jump 48 at 16 m/s); its front flip rides "Pop with: Space".
  - `line.ts <map> <x> <fromD> <toD> [stand|tuck] [pop|nopop] [720] [steer] …`: rides Park-style lines.
  - `face.ts <map> [runs] | --fall N`: Freeride runs and fall lines.
  - `cliffs.ts <map> [--min m] [--caps 12,16,20]` (M15): rides straight over every drop of each cliff band of `--min` m or more, from just above its bench at each speed cap, and prints the air, how far it fell, and the impact against the limit (powder or groomed); a crash is a problem.
  - `course.ts`: slalom courses.
  - `features.ts <map> [kinds]`: every jump, kicker, quarter or jib, ending with a problem count. Since the city on a hill, `handrail` (only when named) pops onto every handrail of its own (not a terrace's: the City's stair sets draw theirs as rails with a 6 m run-on along the plaza). A terrace without stairs or handrails (the City's drops onto banks, M16) is ridden off the middle of its front and beside it; one with them, as before. Its jib runs (5 to 13 m/s) skip 3D rails; the smallest deck rails count one MISSED at 13 m/s on each park (a fast rider flies the 3 m rail).
  - `airbag.ts <map>` (M17): drops the skier onto every airbag's top and face in each crash pose (nose up, on an edge, sideways, too hard, upside down, mid-flip), with the same drop onto plain snow as the control (it should crash there); rides into a fence stood on a pad (a small map built in the tool); then rides every lane from its flag, standing and tucked, with and without a pop and with tricks held, and prints where each landed on the pad. A crash on a pad, a jump that misses its pad or a pad that never lets go is a problem.
  - `avalanche.ts [map] [--lanes n]` (M21, default Freeride): for every zone, rides in tucked from 20 m above its top on a few lanes (every roll a release) and prints where it cracked, whether the rider outran it or crashed (and on what), the closest the front came and the top speed; stands a rider still across the slope partway down and releases above them (caught, and how soon); the same with No crashes (passed over); then rides in 40 times at the real odds. A zone that never sets off, one no tucked lane outruns, or a standing rider not caught is a problem. Today: 0 problems; standing riders are caught 3.4 s after the crack; releases 14 to 33 of 40.
  - `lift.ts <map>` (M17): skis into each lift's gate, rides it to the top (and again, skipping), and prints the wait, the ride, how far the skier sat from the chair's seat and where the top let them off.
  - `loop.ts <map> [--speeds]` (M14): rides every 3D rail from the snow at 8 to 20 m/s and prints, where the rider was most upside down, its speed against what it needed, how far round the up turned, where it let go, and what scoring named; a ride that got round short of the pull, or let go with it covered, is a problem.
  - `jumps.ts`, `kickers.ts`, `butter.ts`, `spin720.ts`.
  - `score.ts`: the scoring check; its rail and butter parts assume Park coordinates. Its very last section (M17) rides Proving Ground's 17 named gaps at 12, 16 and 20 m/s from 16 m before each takeoff area's middle, then a back flip onto each airbag (tagged "Airbag"). Last (after the old summary line, so everything before it stays identical) `rideNamedGaps` rides Freeride's five named gaps at 14, 18 and 22 m/s from 40 m above, then the City's five roof gaps at 12, 16 and 20 m/s from 12 m above (on the takeoff roof: further back, the hops between roofs end the ride first), and last into each of the City's 18 wallrides at 25° and 12 m/s, counting those that score "Wallride". On the Park it also rides milestone 10's rotation cases off jump 75 (turning cheats on and off with `setCheat`) and loads Jam Park for the gap cases (feature indices found by geometry: the open gaps on x = -20, the jump inside the Cat gap's polygon, the tube at x = -64, the fence at x = -42).
- `dev/devHash.ts` (M24, run by the developer in their own terminal): asks for the nickname without echoing it and prints its salted hash (`core/devUnlock.ts devHash`); `--write` puts it in `DEV_HASH`; `--check` asks for the nickname and the code and says only whether either appears in any tracked file, `dist/` or `worker/.dev.vars`.
- Map tools: the generators (one per map) (write `src/maps/<id>.json`), `sinks.ts <map>` (holes a rider can't leave; expect 0), `overview.ts <map> <png> [m/px] [box]` (top-down PNG).

- Model: `model/clearance.ts` (poses vs body; poles off and skating; each headwear; since M22, last, the snowboard rider regular then goofy: every board pose, riding state and push, with the board's top against the body), `model/budget.ts` (the skier's draw calls and triangles for every headwear, pattern and ski, and the worst combination; then the board rider; then (M22 part 2) every board type in every graphic and the worst outfit on each; last (M23) a checkerboard custom board on each type, alone and with the hood and plaid), `model/boardReach.ts` (M22; `boardReach.ts`, `boardBody.ts` and `clearance.ts` take `--board-type <id>` since part 2: every board pose through the rig: each hand on the board or where the pose puts it, each foot in its binding, boots within an ankle's range; expect 0 problems), `model/boardBody.ts [filter] [--skis]` (M22: every board pose and riding state: each knee's angle, the chest's lean, and points of one part inside another by pair, a leg in the torso or the other leg, an arm in the torso or a leg, the head in a limb, the board in the body; the neck's points in the collar apart; `--skis` adds the ski poses to compare), `model/skis.ts [png]` (triangle counts, graphics sheet).
